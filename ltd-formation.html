Good to Know

Before You Order

Transparent pricing

Every price above already includes my service fee — the amount you see is the amount you pay. No surprise charges for what's listed in each package.

Annual renewals

UK companies require yearly renewals (registered office, confirmation statement, accounts). Exact renewal costs are confirmed with you before anything is charged.

KYC verification

Companies House requires ID verification for directors, shareholders, and PSCs. One person's KYC is included from Silver upward; extra people are £30 each.

No guarantees on banks/merchants

I can process and support Stripe, PayPal, and Wise applications on your behalf, but approval decisions are made by those platforms — not by me.
